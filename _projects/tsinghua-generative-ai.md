**July 2024 – June 2025**

I implemented research data-collection infrastructure and synthesized literature on the effects of generative AI.

## My Contributions

- Built a LinkedIn crawler for 7,063 profiles using DrissionPage, depth-first search, multithreading, persistent SQLite storage, and resumable checkpoints.
- Implemented error logging and automated gap recovery for a Google Patents collection pipeline handling approximately 300 GB of Chinese patent text.
- Screened 50 papers and organized 13 core studies into a focused literature review.

**Supervisor:** Prof. Wu Zhu, Tsinghua University.

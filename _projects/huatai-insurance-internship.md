**June – August 2024**

As an intern in the Management Department of Exclusive Agency, I modeled customer value to support segmentation.

## My Contributions

- Used decision trees and regression on one million exclusive-agency customer records to examine predictors of customer value.
- Co-developed a classifier for new customers and segmented existing customers using quadrant-based rules and k-means in R, matching segments to agencies.

**Supervisor:** Lifeng Wang, Manager of Strategy and Innovation Development, Huatai P&C.

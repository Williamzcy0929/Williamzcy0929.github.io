**March – April 2025**

_7th place in Innovation & Creativity._

I developed features and models for sparse, high-dimensional numerical and text data in the Midwest Undergraduate Data Analysis Competition.

## My Contributions

- Encoded sparse, high-dimensional numerical and text data with embeddings, sentiment scores, and pairwise similarities.
- Applied Cox proportional hazards regression and a causal Bayesian network to study match duration and closure.
- Designed a mixture-of-experts model with regression, MLP, and Transformer experts for different feature types and a learned gating network. Compared it with random forest and XGBoost baselines.
- Placed 7th in Innovation & Creativity.

**Supervision:** Barbara Kuzmak and Robert Oliver VandenBurg, University of Minnesota.

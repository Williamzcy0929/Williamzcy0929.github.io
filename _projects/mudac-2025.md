**March – April 2025 · School of Statistics, University of Minnesota, Twin Cities**

_7th in Innovation & Creativity among approximately 200 teams._

I engineered mentoring-match features and modeled match duration and closure with survival analysis, a causal Bayesian network, and a mixture-of-experts model.

## My Contributions

- Engineered features from sparse, high-dimensional numerical and text data for mentoring-match prediction, including embeddings, sentiment scores, and pairwise similarities.
- Modeled match duration and closure using Cox proportional hazards regression and a causal Bayesian network.
- Designed a mixture-of-experts model with regression, MLP, and Transformer experts for different feature types and a learned gating network. Compared it with random forest and XGBoost baselines.
- Placed 7th in Innovation & Creativity among approximately 200 teams at MUDAC 2025.

**Supervision:** Barbara Kuzmak and Robert Oliver VandenBurg, University of Minnesota.

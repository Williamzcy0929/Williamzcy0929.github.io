**March 2024 – May 2025**

_Outstanding Graduation Thesis Award._

**Thesis:** Comparative Analysis of Forecasting Abilities of ARIMA, LSTM, and Transformer Models in the Financial Domain: A Case Study of the Stock Market and Cryptocurrency Market.

My undergraduate thesis compared statistical and neural forecasting models for stock-market and cryptocurrency time series.

## My Contributions

- Collected and harmonized data from Wind and Yahoo Finance; implemented ARIMA in R and LSTM and Transformer models in PyTorch.
- Adapted Transformer input embeddings and feature weighting and compared models across the Shanghai Composite, Nasdaq, and CMC200 indices.

**Supervisor:** Prof. Mengmeng Zheng, Shandong University of Finance and Economics.
